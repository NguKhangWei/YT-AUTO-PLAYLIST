# Privacy Policy

auto-yt is a desktop app you run on your own computer. It has no server and no accounts of its own.

## What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app asks to:

- manage your YouTube account (to add videos to your playlists and list your playlists), and
- read your Google name and profile picture (shown in the app's header).

## Where your data goes

- Your Google sign-in token is stored only on your computer, encrypted with your operating system account. It is only ever sent to Google.
- Your settings (schedulers, tracked channels, run history) are stored only on your computer.
- The app talks only to Google and YouTube. It has no analytics or tracking, and sends nothing to the author or any third party.

## Your control

Sign out in the app, or remove access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, to revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time. Uninstalling the app and deleting its data folder removes everything it stored.
